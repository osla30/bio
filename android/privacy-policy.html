Privacy Policy (English Version)

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for using BioCalculator (科科計算器) ("the App"). We respect your privacy. The App is a 100% offline laboratory calculation toolkit providing 15 calculation tools across Solutions, Nucleic Acids/Primers, Cell Culture, SDS-PAGE Gel Casting, Animal Drug Dosage, and a General Scientific Calculator.

2. Data Collection and Use

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: The App does not require user registration and does not collect, store, access, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.
  • 100% Local Processing: The App does not request internet access permissions (android.permission.INTERNET). All numbers, sequences, and formulas you enter are processed strictly locally on your device and never leave your device.
  • No Ads or Third-Party Trackers: The App contains no advertisements, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking scripts.

3. Device Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive Android permissions. The system clipboard is only used when you explicitly tap a calculator result to copy it.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any data from any user, it fully complies with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the email address listed on the Google Play Store listing page.
